Martha Skin Studio now open in Dix Hills, New York.

The boutique studio is welcoming clients for personalized facials, skincare, makeup, and relaxation-focused services in a calm, private setting.

August 20, 2026

DIX HILLS, N.Y. — Martha Skin Studio is now welcoming clients at 683 Old Country Road, Suite 41, in Dix Hills. Founded by New York State licensed esthetician Martha, the boutique studio offers personalized skincare and beauty services in an inviting environment centered on individual attention, comfort, and trust.

Services include facials, skin rejuvenation, personalized skincare, makeup, scalp massage, and upper-body massage for relaxation. Each visit begins with a conversation about the guest’s preferences and skincare goals, allowing Martha to thoughtfully tailor the experience without relying on a one-size-fits-all approach.

For Martha, opening the studio realizes a longtime dream: creating a warm, peaceful space where guests feel welcomed, heard, and cared for.

Originally from Colombia, Martha has called the United States home for more than 30 years. She brings more than a decade of professional skincare experience to the studio, combining her appreciation for beauty, wellness, and self-care with a personal approach rooted in genuine hospitality.

Her professional qualifications include licensure as a New York State esthetician, certification as a facial specialist and makeup artist, and advanced training in microneedling, microdermabrasion, facial massage, scalp massage, and upper-body massage for relaxation.

Martha Skin Studio welcomes clients from Dix Hills, Huntington, and nearby Long Island communities, including Melville, Commack, Deer Park, and the Half Hollow Hills area.

Visit Martha Skin Studio

The studio is open Tuesday through Saturday from 10 a.m. to 7 p.m. and closed Sunday and Monday. To book an appointment, call 516-660-4411.
